  What I changed (all in aion.py (animation_scripts/aion.py)):

  - Gradiometer continuous scenes were only half-continuous.
    RunsContinuously.close flew the lower cloud's ports, then the upper cloud's,
    as two separate beats. So the upper cloud sat frozen at its vertex while
    the lower one flew, and the clock ran on twice. This affected
    GradiometerGWContinuous, GradiometerGWStretchContinuous and
    LightShiftSignalContinuous. Now all four ports leave together, and the clock
    advance and the sectors happen once.
  - Empty ports froze the clock. draw_ports fades an empty port's dashed guide
    in as its own 0.5 s play before the flight. That's fine in the stop-start
    scenes, but it stops lab time in the continuous ones. It showed up in
    LightShiftSignalContinuous, whose lower cloud sends every atom into one
    port. The guide now fades in during the flight.
  - How: I split draw_ports into port_parts (builds the pieces) and a new
    fly_ports(scene, [(origin, p_ground), ...], length, ...). The new function
    flies every port in one draw_legs call and folds the empty guides into that
    call. DarkMatterPhaseContinuous and RunsContinuously now use fly_ports.

  The stop-start scenes still use draw_ports and behave as before. I didn't
  re-render those. I haven't committed anything, because your working tree has
  other uncommitted changes in aion.py.
